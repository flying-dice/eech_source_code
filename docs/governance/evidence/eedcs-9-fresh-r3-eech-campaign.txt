**When the M4 regression pack differs.** A red pack produces a proposal, never a new baseline:
1. `baseline-governance.py propose` creates a change record listing every delta as `UNCLASSIFIED`.
2. Each delta is classified with evidence as exactly one of:
   - **regression:** rejected, and the candidate stays failing;
   - **approved defect correction;**
   - **intentional semantic change;**
   - **expected input/world variation:** this starts a separate lineage.
3. The PR carries the pack report from before the change, the change record, and the old and proposed baseline identities.
4. The lead approves the commit holding that exact record.
5. A follow-up commit runs `adopt`, which checks the approval on GitHub. It adds the new version and keeps the old one. That commit needs fresh approval.

Baselines are never re-recorded in place, and tolerances are never widened to make a candidate pass.
